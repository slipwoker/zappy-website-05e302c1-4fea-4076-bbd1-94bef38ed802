Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What areas of California do you serve?

I specialize in California's most prestigious coastal and urban markets, including Beverly Hills, Malibu, Santa Barbara, Newport Beach, and the San Francisco Bay Area. My residential real estate expertise spans the full spectrum of California's luxury corridor.

Do you represent buyers as well as sellers?

Absolutely. I offer dedicated buyer representation with full fiduciary care — from curating private property previews and negotiating on your behalf to guiding you through every step of due diligence. Your interests are always my singular priority.

Can you assist international buyers?

Yes. I regularly work with international clients acquiring condos in California and private villas as primary residences, vacation homes, or investment assets. I coordinate with cross-border legal counsel and financial advisors to ensure a seamless acquisition experience regardless of your home country.

What types of properties do you specialize in?

My focus is exclusively on high-end condos and private villas — architecturally significant residences that command a premium for their design, location, and lifestyle amenities. I do not handle standard residential transactions, which allows me to offer truly specialist-level knowledge in the luxury tier.

How do you communicate with your clients?

I tailor communication to each client's preference — whether that's concise email updates, scheduled calls, or real-time messaging. You will always have direct access to me personally. I believe that at this level of real estate services, responsiveness and discretion are non-negotiable.

Are you affiliated with the Department of Real Estate?

Yes. I am fully licensed by the California Department of Real Estate and operate in complete compliance with all state regulations. You can trust that every transaction is conducted with the highest standards of professional ethics, transparency, and legal integrity.
