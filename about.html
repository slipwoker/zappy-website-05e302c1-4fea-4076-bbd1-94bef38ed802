About Sienna

California's Trusted Advisor for Prestigious Real Estate

Sienna Rhodes brings over a decade of expertise to California's most coveted residential real estate market. Born and raised along the California coast, she developed an intimate understanding of the state's distinct neighborhoods — from the sun-drenched bluffs of Malibu to the architectural jewels of Pacific Heights. Her practice is built on discretion, precision, and an unwavering commitment to her clients' vision of home.

Specializing exclusively in high-end condos and private villas, Sienna has guided discerning buyers and sellers through some of California's most significant transactions. As a licensed member of the Department of Real Estate, she combines rigorous market intelligence with a deeply personal approach — ensuring every client feels both informed and genuinely cared for throughout the process.
